In the laste decennies, comune speche of craft and engynes hath ofte y-turned upon ful knowen weyes: sum folk holden an over-mery hope, othere drede a derk mischaunce, and yet othere, in ful hevy wisdom, trowen that such chaunges ben but an unstaunchable force of kynde. Nathelees, bihynde these voisës of the peple lyeth a subtile and privy parodox—oon that toucheth how men shapen her jugement whan the toolës they trusten waxen swifter in chaunging than the wit by which they ben y-understonden. This parodox is nat but philosophye aloone; it stretcheth into marchaundyse, governaunce of folk, and even the privy choisës of ech man and womman. Thus semeth the verray peril of technologie nat the hast of innouacioun, but the sloweliche turning of mannes owne thought.

Loke, for ensaumple, upon the craft of predictive engynes, y-set in heling-houses for lechecraft diagnosys, and in the market-places for speedy cheuysaunce. Frendes of such craft seyn that these engynes shenden doute, for they sen patternës to dere for mortal eyen. Yet, in an hard parodox, the brode usage of these engynes **maketh** newe doute. Leches, standing by strong AI for sickness-reading, mote now deme whan to trust the werk of the engyn, and whan to ouer-ryde it—deming that asketh nat oonly craft of lechehood, but craft of knowing bias of engyns, of data that slideth out of kynde, and of subtile adversarie wyles. In the world of marchantz, too, automatik trede-engynes mowe breke markets with chainës of chaunging that no man may trace to oon singulere will, but rather to the derk intrelacing of multitudinous codës.

Here riseth the strif from a ful hard treuthe: **men ofte trowen they witen more of the toolës they forge than sooth it is.** And the more folk leanen upon engyns of gret entanglement, the more dredful the harm whan these engyns ben mys-y-taken. This hath y-appeered in the art of flyght, where pilots, lerned in olde governaille, hadden sore trauaile to acorde with newe automatik help-engyns—wanhope that brougte forth grete mischaunces blazed in alle landës. Tragically, the pilots were blamed for failing to amend the same engyns that were wroght to amend hem.

Yet, doute is nat alweyes a cursed thing. Through the ages, folk of many landës han y-found weyes to suffer the storm of the unknowe: in olde tyme with mythes and godly tales, and in modern day with statutes and bordës of rule. What semeth newe in oure age is the **swiftne** with which doute is born. For whan folk had score-yeres to meet with the chaunges of craft—be it smithing of yren or coming of the net of webs—normës mighte arise by slow and un-even bargain. But now, whan newe engyns—grete modellës, strange encryptioun, or wondrous biocraft—springen forth ech few monthes, folk live nat deliberatly but improvisen, patching her worlde as best they mowe.

This improvisacioun hath begotten that which clerkës call **“fatigue of an-ticipacioun.”** Folk ben nat fallen low by technologie itself, but by the euer-pressing charge that they mote stand ready for a future that shapeth itself without warnyng. Yong scolers wonden what craft shal kepe hem in labour ten yeres hence. Elders drede that newe devices mowe steal privité from her children, in weyes no law hath yet y-spoken of. Makers of statutes stryven to write newe ruleës that ben nat as dust ere they ben printed.

The parodox groweth more strange whan we consider how doute shimeth nat oonly in choosing among technologies, but in choices **y-shaped by** the same technologies. Communal webs speeden the reward to tho that gather eyen rather than trouth; way-finding engyns move the very stretes with their counsel; recommaundynge engyns turnen folkës likyng and herte’s desyr or ever they knowe it hemself. Here, men nat oonly answeren to doute—they answere to engyns that taken her olde deedës as seed for newe rule. Thus is y-mad a “soft determinacioun,” wherein free wille abideth, yet is lad by stille and un-seen hand.
